# A quiet morning

Some ideas need a little room to grow.

Open a page. Follow a thought. Let the words find their shape.

## Make space for a thought

Write **what matters**, keep a few *unfinished ideas*, and leave the rest for another day. Your notes stay in plain Markdown, in a folder you choose.

> Less between you and the page.
